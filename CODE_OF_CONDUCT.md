# Code of Conduct

Lumen is a welcoming project. Everyone who takes part, whether by opening issues, reviewing code, commenting or contributing, is expected to help keep it that way.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is how it applies here; the full text is the reference.

## Our standards

Things that make the community better:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including when you disagree
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully
- Assuming good intent and helping newcomers find their way
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, such as issues, pull requests and Discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or contact the maintainer, [@sabbirrifat](https://github.com/sabbirrifat). Reports are handled privately and as quickly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on severity, responses range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban from the project, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
